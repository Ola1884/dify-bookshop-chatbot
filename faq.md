# FAQ — Kawn Bookshop

## 1. What are your opening hours?

We’re open **Saturday–Thursday, 10:00 AM–8:00 PM**, and **Friday, 2:00 PM–9:00 PM**. We may close early on public holidays and adjust hours during Ramadan; updates are posted on our social media.

## 2. Where are you located?

We’re at **12 El-Maahad El-Eshteraki Street, Heliopolis, Cairo**. We’re a short walk from the nearest metro station, and there’s limited street parking nearby.

## 3. Do you ship books?

Yes. We ship across Egypt via courier:

- **Cairo and Giza:** 2–5 business days
- **Elsewhere in Egypt:** 3–7 business days
- **Delivery fee:** EGP 60
- **Free delivery:** On orders over EGP 1,500

International shipping is available by quote only—please contact us before ordering.

## 4. What is your return policy?

Unused books in original condition can be returned within **14 days** with a receipt for a refund or exchange.

Damaged or incorrect books will be exchanged or refunded if reported within **48 hours** of delivery.

Special orders are non-refundable unless the book is defective or we ordered the wrong title.

## 5. What payment methods do you accept?

We accept:

- Cash
- Credit/debit cards
- Vodafone Cash
- InstaPay
- Bank transfer

We do not accept cheques. For special orders, a deposit may be required.

## 6. What languages do you stock?

Our main sections are **Arabic and English**. We also carry a small selection of **French and German** titles, plus children’s books in multiple languages.

If we don’t have a title in the language you need, we can usually try to order it.

## 7. Can I place a special order for a book you don’t have?

Yes. Send us the title, author, ISBN if available, and preferred language/edition via email, WhatsApp, or our in-store request form.

Most orders arrive in **1–3 weeks**. A deposit may be required for expensive or prepaid titles. If a book cannot be sourced, any deposit is refunded.
